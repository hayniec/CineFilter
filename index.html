Movie Database API & Architecture Guide

Answering your questions on IMDb, TMDB, and live movie discovery

⚠️

1. The Truth About the IMDb API

IMDb (Internet Movie Database) is owned by Amazon and does not provide an open, free REST API. Official commercial licenses cost upwards of thousands of dollars per month via AWS Data Exchange. Third-party scraping APIs (on RapidAPI) exist, but have restrictive daily limits (often ~100 calls) and frequently break.

🚀

2. The Live Solution: TMDB + OMDb

This app is connected directly to The Movie Database (TMDB) and OMDb out of the box, granting you access to over 800,000+ real movies with:

  • Exact Runtime Filtering: with_runtime.lte
  • Multi-Genre Checkboxes: with_genres=28,35
  • MPA Age Certifications: certification.lte=PG-13
  • Actor & Year: with_cast & primary_release_year
  • IMDb Integration: Official IMDb IDs and direct links for every title!
🛡️

3. VidAngel & ClearPlay Integration

VidAngel and ClearPlay filter streaming platforms (Apple TV+, Netflix, Amazon Prime). Since they don't have public APIs, this application:

  • Provides 1-click direct title searches on VidAngel and ClearPlay
  • Maintains curated filter tags and content sensitivity meters (profanity, violence, nudity, substances)
  • Links directly to IMDb's official Parents Guide for detailed scene-by-scene content advisories
